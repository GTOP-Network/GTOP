# Figure 6 generation
***
This directory contains the scripts for generating Figure 6:
* Figure 6A: GWAS enrichment of cis-eQTLs and cis-sQTLs measured with four approaches.
* Figure 6B: Stacked bars show the number of colocalized GWAS loci by eQTL, sQTL, and both, ordered by trait domain. Venn diagram shows the number of SuSiE-coloc and SMR detected genes.
* Figure 6C: Proportion of GTOP colocalized genes compared with those from GTEx, the JCTF, and MAGE, using the same SuSiE-coloc and GWAS data.
* Figure 6D: LocusCompare plot illustrating the colocalization between ADAP1 cis-eQTLs and Crohn's disease in GTOP, but not in GTEx.
* Figure 6E: Enrichment of QTLs identified by GTOP for associations with GWAS signals from TPMI and GWAS catalog European populations.
* Figure 6F: Proportion of GWAS risk variants overlapping with QTL variants, stratified by variant frequency across ancestry groups.
* Figure 6G: Upset plot characterizes the number of colocalized signals containing fine-mapped SV/TRs.
* Figure 6H: LocusZoom plots for hyperlipidemia GWAS signal and HP SNV-sQTL signal and SV-sQTL signal, colored by LD with rs77303550.
